FAQs

Frequently Asked
Questions 🤔

Got questions? We've got answers! If you don't find what you're looking for,
feel free to reach out to us directly.

My child is only 4 years old — is chess not too early for them?
+
Not at all! Age 4 is actually a wonderful time to start chess. Our beginner program uses stories, colorful pieces, and games to introduce chess concepts in a way that's completely natural for a 4-year-old brain. Children this age are incredibly receptive to learning structured games when taught the right way!
Do my child and I need any prior chess knowledge?
+
Zero prior knowledge needed! Our beginner program starts from absolute scratch — we teach the names of pieces, how they move, and simple rules in the most fun way possible. All you need is a device and an internet connection!
How many students are in each batch?
+
We strictly limit batches to a maximum of 2 students per class. This ensures every child receives personal attention from the coach, can ask questions freely, and never feels lost or overlooked. Your child will truly be seen and heard in every session!
Can I get a free trial class before enrolling?
+
Absolutely! We offer a FREE 1-hour trial class for all new students. Just fill out the enrollment form and mention "Free Trial" in your message — our team will schedule a convenient slot for your child within 24 hours. No credit card required!
What is the duration of each online class?
+
Each group & buddy session for online class is for 55 mins while the individual session is for 45 minutes.
What happens if my child misses a class?
+
If your child misses a class without prior cancellation, the session count will be deducted. However, if you cancel the class in advance (atleast 1 hour prior), the class will be carry forwarded without reducing your eligible count.
What happens when my online course is finished?
+
You will be provided with a course completion certificate! We strongly recommend students to go for advanced levels with us after completing a 12 month course.
